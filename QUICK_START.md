# Quick Start Guide - Pack Feature

## What's New?

Your Family Feud game now has **Packs** - think of them as different game sessions that remember their scores!

## Quick Example

```
Pack A: "Team Lazarus" vs "Team Fishcake"
├── Session 1: Name popular pizza toppings
├── Session 2: Name things in a classroom
└── Last Score: Team Lazarus 150, Team Fishcake 200 ✅

Pack B: "Red Dragons" vs "Blue Phoenixes"  
├── Session 1: Name types of fruit
├── Session 2: Name countries in Europe
└── Not played yet
```

## How to Use (3 Simple Steps)

### 1. Setup Page - Create Your Packs

1. Open `index.html`
2. Click **"Add Pack"** (top of page)
3. Click the pack header to expand it
4. Type in your team names:
   - **Team 1**: "Team Lazarus"
   - **Team 2**: "Team Fishcake"
5. Click **"Add Session"** and add your questions

### 2. Start a Pack

**Option A - Play One Pack:**
- Click the orange **"Start This Pack"** button on any pack
- Goes straight to the game with your team names

**Option B - Choose Later:**
- Click **"Start Game"** at the bottom
- Pick which pack to play from the selection screen

### 3. See the Scores

After playing, you'll see:
- Winner announcement with your team names
- Final scores for both teams
- When you return to setup, a **golden banner** shows the last score

## Cool Features

✨ **Each pack tracks its own score**  
✨ **Play packs in any order**  
✨ **Team names appear everywhere** (game, results, winner announcement)  
✨ **Scores saved automatically** when game ends  
✨ **Perfect for tournaments** - create one pack per round

## Score Display Example

When you expand a pack that's been played, you'll see:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Last Score: Team Lazarus: 150 | Team Fishcake: 200 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

This golden banner shows you the results from the last time you played this pack!

## Tips

💡 **Create all packs at once** - Set up your whole tournament before playing  
💡 **Descriptive team names** - "Red Team" is better than "Team 1"  
💡 **One pack per match** - Great for tracking series/tournaments  
💡 **Reuse packs** - Play the same pack multiple times, score updates each time
